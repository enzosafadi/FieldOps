# FieldOps AI

### Workforce, Quality & Delivery Operations

A portfolio project exploring how distributed AI data collection teams can connect staffing, technical readiness, quality assurance, delivery commitments, and direct costs in one operational workflow.

**Status: project definition.** Application development has not started. There is no deployed demo or measured performance result yet.

**Independent simulation:** fictional organizations and simulated operational data; no affiliation with a client or employer is implied.

## The business problem

Having enough workers on a schedule does not guarantee accepted work by a deadline. Absence, connectivity failures, rejected submissions, and review queues can all reduce deliverable output. FieldOps AI is designed around the manager's decision: identify the constraint, choose a feasible action, and evaluate the consequences.

## Planned demonstration

A connectivity outage reduces collection capacity. A manager investigates an at-risk commitment, reviews qualified available workers, and records a reassignment. The simulation evaluates accepted delivery, QA capacity, and direct cost against the same baseline conditions.

## Evidence standard

Reproducible simulated inputs, explicit metric definitions, valid assignment constraints, and traceable manager actions. Future results will be labeled as simulated and published only after validation.
